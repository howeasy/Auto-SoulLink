Patch capabilities

The patch provides cartridge hooks for these features. Availability also depends on the connected game and runtime; applying a patch does not by itself confirm readiness.

{% if target.slug == 'rr' %}
  • Native message box — momentous overworld events (a link forms, a shiny is found, an area becomes a dead zone) show in the real in-game text box instead of the HUD.
  • Peer ghost — your partner walks your overworld as a real NPC, rendered as their own trainer avatar & colours, moving in real time.
  • Talk to your partner — face the ghost + press A for a native Trade / Say hey menu. Trade plays the real in-game trade scene on both sides (including trade-evolution); Say hey pings your partner.
  • Native sound — link / KO / shiny cues play through the game's own sound engine instead of a Lua RAM-poke.
  • Bundled Battle Calc — an in-battle damage / type-effectiveness readout for the highlighted move, folded into the same patch.
