# Brand and visual identity

Source: OMP research report `cx-ed94d6ce` (SECOND_OPINION, read-only, repo at master a9bdce38).

## Summary

Keep the pixel identity as a display and graphic accent, not the public site's body face. Replace
the Poké Ball-derived mark with an original twin-link symbol. Follow the system light/dark
preference. Ship a compact icon set. Self-host only sprite assets whose rights are cleared.

The report's own strongest doubt: The Pokémon Company's support policy asks people not to use or
associate Pokémon IP with their projects, and the PokéAPI sprite repository's CC0 disclaims
trademark rights and non-infringement. Removing the ball mark lowers the obvious risk; it does not
make a public fan site risk-free.

## Legal and IP facts that affect current choices

These are facts from the cited sources, not legal advice.

> **1. The bundled Radical Red sprites come from a repository with no licence.**
> `server/static/sprites/rr/` (1,322 PNGs at master a9bdce38) is generated by
> `tools/gen_rr_sprites.py`, which extracts them from `JwowSquared/Radical-Red-Pokedex`'s
> `data.js` (`tools/gen_rr_sprites.py:1-13`). That repository declares no licence; GitHub's API
> reports `license: null`:
> [repository metadata](https://api.github.com/repos/JwowSquared/Radical-Red-Pokedex).
> No written permission is on record. The report recommends not publishing this bundle until its
> licensing is established.
>
> **2. The Pokémon Company's support page asks people not to use its IP.** Its US support answer
> says it cannot review requests to use Pokémon characters, names or designs, and asks people not
> to use them "in or associate [them] with your project in any way":
> [official response, updated 2026-01-30](https://support.pokemon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000634094-Can-I-use-Pok%C3%A9mon-images-or-materials).

Related facts from the same report:

- Nintendo Australia says names, logos, characters, graphics and symbols are protected, permission is
  required, silence is not permission, and lists Pokémon among its trademarks:
  [Nintendo Intellectual Property](https://www.nintendo.com/au/legal/nintendo-intellectual-property/).
  This is regional policy.
- PokéAPI's sprite licence says image contents are copyright The Pokémon Company, the repo is CC0
  1.0, and no trademark or patent rights are waived, with no non-infringement warranty:
  [image licence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/sprites/master/LICENCE.txt),
  [CC0 itself](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en). CC0 is not a
  blanket Pokémon permission.
- Fan projects cited below are examples, not permission or a legal safe harbour.

## Key findings

### 1. Typography: keep the pixel character, move it out of body copy

Importance high; confidence high on licences, phone rendering not exercised.

- Pixelify Sans is the default UI and body face, and Jersey 20 the whole board face:
  `server/static/slink.css:128-138`, `server/static/board.css:22-30`. Board text drops to about
  12-14px, where pixel glyphs suffer most.
- Upstream metadata lists both as display fonts:
  [Pixelify Sans](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/pixelifysans/METADATA.pb),
  [Jersey 20](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/jersey20/METADATA.pb).
  (Open: the validator did not confirm this category.)
- Licences: [Pixelify Sans OFL 1.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eifetx/Pixelify-Sans/master/OFL.txt),
  [Jersey 20 OFL 1.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scfried/soft-type-jersey/master/OFL.txt),
  [Atkinson Hyperlegible OFL 1.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/googlefonts/atkinson-hyperlegible/main/OFL.txt).
  Atkinson was designed for low-vision recognition:
  [primary description](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Atkinson+Hyperlegible/about).
- `server/static/fonts/` holds five font binaries and no OFL notice; the CSS comment points at a
  missing `VERSIONS.md` (`server/static/slink.css:21-27`). OFL redistribution should include the
  copyright and licence text.

Proposed type system:

- **Display:** Pixelify Sans 600/700 at 24px and up; wordmark, page titles, short headings.
- **Body/UI:** Atkinson Hyperlegible 400/700, 16px minimum, line-height 1.5-1.65.
- **Large figures:** Jersey 20 only at about 20px or larger.
- **Technical values:** native monospace, not Press Start 2P.
- Carry the retro feel through sprites, hard 1px borders, stepped shadows, 4px spacing and bitmap
  ornaments, not pervasive pixel text.
- Ship all three OFL notices beside the font files.

[WCAG 1.4.4](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/resize-text.html),
[WCAG 1.4.12](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/text-spacing.html).

### 2. The mark: the current ball is the largest avoidable risk

Importance critical; confidence high that the mark is the avoidable centrepiece of the risk,
medium on legal exposure without counsel.

- The rail mark is a Poké Ball construction: circle, horizontal band, centre opening, red top,
  black lower field (`server/templates/_rail.html:10-15`).
- Comparable projects keep "Pokémon" as plain descriptive text and use their own mark:
  [Pokémon Showdown](https://pokemonshowdown.com/) (its
  [MIT licence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown/master/README.md) covers
  software, not Pokémon IP); [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co/) (its
  [software licence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/master/LICENSE.md) names
  Nintendo trademarks and prohibits endorsement claims);
  [Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Main_Page) (its
  [logo file page](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:Bulbapedia_1x_logo.png) restricts
  reuse); [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg/) (Pokémon listed among supported games, not the
  master brand); [Pokémon Randomizer](https://pokemonrandomizer.net/).

Proposed original mark:

- Two mirrored, angular open chain-link brackets interlocking at the centre.
- Cyan and SLink gold, not red, white and black.
- Negative-space "S" or "SL" at the seam.
- Square or chamfered: not circular, no full-width band, no centre button.
- A solid silhouette legible at 16px.

Wordmark "SLink". "Pokémon" only in plain descriptive text, never in the logo, favicon or social
card. Add an independent-project notice, knowing a disclaimer is not permission.

### 3. Favicon, manifest and Open Graph

Importance high for launch polish; confidence high.

The shared head has no icon, manifest, theme-color or social metadata
(`server/templates/base.html:3-12`). No spec mandates a favicon size; MDN documents `rel="icon"`,
`sizes`, SVG `sizes="any"` and ICO as the legacy fallback
([MDN `<link>` reference](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/link#rel_icon)).
Chromium installability needs 192px and 512px
([MDN installability requirements](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Progressive_web_apps/Guides/Making_PWAs_installable)).

Asset set:

- `favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48); `favicon.svg` (`sizes="any"`); `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`.
- `apple-touch-icon-180.png`.
- `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` (`purpose: "any"`); `icon-maskable-512.png` (`purpose: "maskable"`,
  opaque background, mark inside the central 80%, per [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/maskable-icon)).
- Optional monochrome `safari-pinned-tab.svg`.
- `site.webmanifest` as `application/manifest+json` with `name`, `short_name`, `id`, `start_url`,
  `scope`, `display: "standalone"`, `prefer_related_applications: false`, description and colours
  ([web.dev manifest guide](https://web.dev/articles/add-manifest),
  [W3C manifest specification](https://w3c.github.io/manifest/)). Manifest `theme_color` cannot vary
  by media, so use two media-specific `theme-color` meta tags
  ([web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/add-manifest#theme_color_in_media_queries)).

Open Graph: one 1200×630 PNG or JPEG under 8MB, which is Facebook's recommendation, not an OG
mandate ([Facebook image-sharing requirements](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/sharing/webmasters/images));
`og:title`, `og:type`, `og:url`, `og:image`, plus description, site name, image type, width, height
and alt ([Open Graph protocol](https://ogp.me/)); absolute HTTPS image URL. The site card uses the
twin-link mark and an abstract pattern, with no ball geometry and no third-party sprite art.

(`spectator-share.md` proposes per-run preview images that do include sprites. That is a separate
decision tied to the sprite rights question.)

### 4. Themes: system preference by default, three public choices

Importance high; confidence high.

- The picker exposes eight themes: Default (dark), Light, Grape, Jungle, Fire, Ice, Watermelon,
  Smoke (`server/templates/_theme_switcher.html:18-27`).
- The server whitelist has nine, adding `transparent` for stream overlays
  (`server/templating.py:46-56`). With no query or cookie it always picks `default` dark
  (`server/templating.py:66-85`).
- `CONTRAST.md` puts Light's alive and pending accents at 3.3:1 and 3.6:1 and records a 1.57:1
  Smoke brand exception (`server/static/themes/CONTRAST.md:16-40`). See `mobile-a11y.md` for the
  later contrast review that found `CONTRAST.md` stale.

Public phase 1 exposes **Auto/system**, **Light**, **Dark** (the current Default palette). OS dark
gives dark; OS light or no preference gives light
([`prefers-color-scheme`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/prefers-color-scheme)).
An explicit choice overrides and persists. Set `color-scheme` and media-specific `theme-color`.
Keep all eight palettes for the Manager and OBS; never expose `transparent` publicly; never let hue
alone carry status.

### 5. Sprites: self-host for reliability; the licence is a claim, not a trademark safe harbour

Importance critical before launch; confidence high on hosting, medium on the legal sufficiency of
CC0.

- Adapters hot-link mutable `master` URLs: `server/adapters/base.py:482-490`,
  `server/adapters/gen1_rby.py:377-379`, `server/adapters/gen2_crystal.py:316-318`,
  `server/adapters/gen4_hgsspt.py:304-307`, `server/adapters/gen5_bw.py:189-196`; badges
  `server/templates/stream/_badges_root.html:12-15`.
- The PokéAPI sprites README says images are hosted outside the API and recommends downloading the
  collection ([sprites README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PokeAPI/sprites/master/README.md)).
- PokéAPI's JSON API has no numeric rate limit and asks for local caching
  ([fair-use policy](https://pokeapi.co/docs/v2#overview)); that policy does not cover
  `raw.githubusercontent.com`. GitHub may throttle excessive bandwidth
  ([GitHub Acceptable Use Policies](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies#excessive-bandwidth-use));
  its 60 requests/hour figure is for the REST API, not raw content.
- The RR sprites: see the legal box above.

Recommendation: build-time import from a pinned upstream commit, never runtime `master`; record
source commit, filename mapping, hashes, licence snapshot and date; serve versioned immutable
assets from SLink's own storage; explicit dimensions and lazy loading; a credits page with source,
licence as supplied, The Pokémon Company copyright notice and a non-affiliation statement. If the RR
position cannot be resolved for phase 1, use text, species IDs or a neutral original placeholder.

## Recommendations

**P0**

1. Replace the mark before launch: commission the angular twin-link symbol; remove the ball from
   the rail, favicon, manifest and social card; wordmark "SLink"; Pokémon only in plain text; add a
   non-affiliation notice.
2. Complete an IP inventory: every Pokémon mark, sprite family, repo licence, upstream owner and
   commercial or marketing use. Do not publish the RR sprite bundle until its rights are
   established.
3. Split retro from readable type: Pixelify display only, Atkinson body and UI, Jersey for large
   figures; ship each OFL licence; test 320 and 375px at 200% and 400% zoom.

**P1**

4. The browser identity set (ICO, SVG, PNG 16/32, Apple 180, manifest 192/512 plus maskable 512,
   `site.webmanifest`, 1200×630 card).
5. Public themes Auto, Light and Dark; persist explicit choices; keep other palettes internal; never
   expose `transparent`.
6. Self-host cleared sprites: pinned import, versioned URLs, immutable caching, credits, hashes,
   lazy loading; remove runtime `master` hot-links.
7. A fan-project disclosure: independent, not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Nintendo,
   Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company, without implying the notice is permission.

## Where the report expects disagreement

- Keeping the ball mark with a disclaimer.
- Pixelify or Jersey as 12-16px body text.
- All eight themes as public defaults.
- "PokéAPI sprites are CC0, therefore safe."
- Applying PokéAPI's API policy or GitHub's REST limit to raw sprite delivery.
- Hot-linking because the repo exists for image hosting (mutable branch, privacy leak, availability
  risk).

## Unverified and open

- **Validator open item:** whether both Pixelify Sans and Jersey 20 are in Google Fonts' display
  category was not confirmed.
- Jurisdictional clearance of the name, copy, sprite use, monetization and advertising.
- Trademark availability of the proposed twin-link mark; no search performed.
- PokéAPI's authority to license every image under CC0; CC0 cannot clear rights the affirmer does
  not own.
- Radical Red sprite rights: no licence found; permission unresolved.
- No authoritative numeric limit for raw GitHub content.
- Phone typography at 200% and 400% zoom not exercised.
- Generated icons and social cards not rendered in browsers or crawlers.

6 accepted, 1 open. A Sonnet validator confirmed 6 of 6: the Poké Ball rail mark; no OFL files in
`server/static/fonts`; `gen_rr_sprites` sourcing from JwowSquared; TPC's support page asking

disclaimers; and JwowSquared's licence being null. Open: whether both fonts are in Google Fonts'
