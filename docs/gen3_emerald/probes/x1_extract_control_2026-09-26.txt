Implementation contract
-----------------------
tools/extract_expansion_data.py reads GF at ROM 0x100 and scans 0x100..0x400
for unique RHHEXP magic. GF version/language must be English Emerald (3/2).
RHH supported version is 1.17.0; counts come from RHH, never layout counts.
GF sources: pret and expansion src/rom_header_gf.c:18-105. RHH source:
expansion src/rom_header_rhh.c:14-28 (explicit public-ABI offsets).

Per-build layout is JSON schema 1, ROM-SHA1-bound, with provenance and separate
table strides, field offsets/widths/counts/bit shifts/bit widths/signedness,
inline/external/pointer names, evolution layout, terminator and ignored methods.
See tool module docstring and vanilla_layout() for schema; the synthetic test's
layout exercises expansion shapes but is explicitly NOT real expansion geometry.
Counts include the zero record and unused slots, preserving ROM IDs.
Names preserve ROM text/case and symbolic glyphs, e.g. {POKEBLOCK}; no prettifying.
Families use connected evolution components, preferring the lowest unevolved
root (Pichu 172, not Pikachu 25). A rootless cycle uses its minimum ID. Raw
method/parameter/target edges remain in the pack. No later-generation baby
species or form relationships are invented. National dex is read, not inferred
from species IDs. Types and abilities retain numeric engine IDs, including 0.

Vanilla struct sources: pret include/pokemon.h:297-338,355-360 and
include/item.h:10-26. Strides 28/12/44/13 (species/moves/items/ability names) and
evolution stride 8 are checked against published .sym sizes. Counts are parsed
from pret include/constants/{species.h:420,moves.h:360,items.h:420,abilities.h:83};
EVOS_PER_MON=5 from constants/pokemon.h:254. Name lengths are parsed from
constants/global.h:94-108. NUM_SPECIES=412 excludes Egg at 412 but includes
unused old-Unown slots 252-276 (constants/species.h:257-281,418-420).

HEADER boundary: GF lacks evolution/national-dex pointers. Only those two use
supplemental SOURCE addresses from the pinned symbol artifact:
  sSpeciesToNationalPokedexNum 0x0831dc82, size 0x336 (.sym:40421)
  gEvolutionTable             0x0832531c, size 0x4060 (.sym:40912)
