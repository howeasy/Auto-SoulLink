Diagnosis (product/binding, not instrument): with lua/gen3/run.lua loaded, and so with its
event.on_bus_exec signal hooks registered, every RR frame end lands on the BIOS IRQ vector in IRQ mode.
It never lands in the halted System-mode park that the pack admits. hello_ready() therefore never
passes, and no RR duo row can get past hello. The same predicate with no hooks admits from
frame 635. FR/LG are unaffected because their park is the game's own ROM WaitForVBlank busy loop
(thumb, mode 31), not the BIOS halt. As far as the probes in docs/gen3/probes show, this is the first
live RR hello on the new client.
Needs an owner/coordinator call, because the overworld predicate is G3-signed "VERBATIM". There are two options:
admit the BIOS IRQ-entry park for RR (R15 in BIOS, mode 0x12), or re-census RR's park with the client's
hooks registered.
