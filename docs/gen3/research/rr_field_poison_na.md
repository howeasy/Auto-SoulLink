# RR field poison is N/A, not unpinned: DoPoisonFieldEffect is a no-op stub

Card RR-POISON-NA, 2026-09-27. Clean Radical Red 4.1, SHA-1 `964f951a0fdaf209e4ea1344883ef0d557bb3a80` (`data/games/gen3_rr/profile.json:399`, `tools/pin_gen3_site.py:20-22`). **On the admitted RR cartridge, overworld field poison cannot damage or faint a party mon at all, so SLink's `poison_hp_before` (+0x34) and `poison_faint` (+0x4E) rows are N/A on RR rather than undiscovered.** The entry keeps the vanilla symbol address `0x080A0618` — `DoPoisonFieldEffect`, 0x82 bytes, `data/gen3/pret/pokefirered.sym:6887`, the authority for the address, as for every FRLG row — but RR replaces the body with a Thumb literal-load trampoline (`LDR Rx,[pc,#imm]*4` / `BX Rx`); `tools/pin_gen3_site.py:54` `decode_thumb_detour` decodes exactly that shape, refuses any other, and reads the target out of the entry's own literal pool rather than assuming one. It yields **`0x090B20D4`** (flat `0x010B20D4`), and the four bytes there are **`00 20 70 47`** = `MOVS r0,#0` (halfword `0x2000`) / `BX lr` (halfword `0x4770`). The stub is two instructions: it returns immediately, so it cannot contain the party loop, the `GetMonData(MON_DATA_HP)` read, or the `SetMonData` HP store the vanilla capture offsets were measured against — there is no HP mutation anywhere on this path, and no before/after pair to pair a faint with. (`r0 = 0` is read elsewhere in the repo as `FLDPSN_NONE`, `tools/gen3_fixtures.py:1730`; that constant's spelling is the repo's reading of pret `src/field_poison.c`, not a byte this note measured.) The consumer is `tools/gen_gen3_engine_signals.py:804-816` `rr_resolution`, which decodes the same detour and classifies both rows UNVERIFIED with "NO HP mutation" as the reason, so `data/games/gen3_rr/engine_signals.json` emits no poison site for either the `clean` or the `companion` artifact and no capture offset is ever armed. CFRU `src/overworld.c:1934-2001` has the `NO_POISON_IN_OW` / `POISON_1_HP_SURVIVAL` branches that explain the intent (`docs/gen3_engine_sites.md:15`), but the four bytes are what settles it: the binary has no HP mutation to find. Consequence downstream: the `poison_faint_gen3` duo scenario stays refused on RR rather than "to be found" — nothing to build a fixture around (`tools/e2e_duo.py` `SCENARIOS`, `tools/gen3_fixtures.py:1728-1736`, `tests/unit/test_e2e_duo_scenario_selection.py:1171-1175`), and `docs/gen3_requirements.md:110` row S-11 is ◐ FR/LG PINNED / RR N/A, not a pending gap. Pinned by `tests/unit/test_gen3_rr_field_poison_na.py`, which measures the four bytes on the clean dump, checks the UNVERIFIED label is *derived* from them (perturbing the stub in a copy moves the classification), and asserts neither RR artifact's site table names poison.

## Reverting this claim

Changing the conclusion needs a byte change, not a source reading: if the clean dump's bytes at `0x090B20D4` stop being `00 20 70 47`, the detour decode in `decode_thumb_detour` still names the target but the body is no longer a no-op, and `rr_resolution`'s reason flips to "poison disabled-body evidence differs; do not reuse vanilla tail". That flip is the revert-check. A change to the trampoline at `0x080A0618` itself (not a `LDR`/`BX`-same-register pair, or a different literal) raises out of `decode_thumb_detour` instead. Either way the generator's own refusal stands until someone re-pins the body deliberately.
