# SLink Gen 1 execution and observation

Language for the R/B/Y Soul Link's native execution and coherent inventory observations.

## Language

**Native command lease**:
A native trade, partner prompt or receptionist operation's claim on execution until its terminal state. A completed local receipt does not necessarily end that claim while the other participant's decision or release is pending.
_Avoid_: Command acknowledgment (an acknowledgment is not the lease).

**Writer hold**:
A claim that gameplay must remain stopped while inventory is captured or a held write is performed. Releasing this claim does not release another owner's hold.

**Inventory checkpoint**:
A coherent observation of the player's inventory at one emulated frame.

**Native checkpoint**:
The native party observation paired with an inventory checkpoint from the same held frame. Absence of a party observation is not evidence of a completed trade.
